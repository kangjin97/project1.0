EasyPlan Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

EasyPlan helps friends collect ideas for things to do, share them in groups and plan them together. This page explains what the app stores, who can see it, and how to delete it.

What we store

  • Account: your email address and password (stored securely, never readable by us), username, display name, bio, profile picture and time zone.
  • Signing in with Google: if you choose Continue with Google, Google tells us your name, email address and profile picture, and confirms it's you. We never see your Google password. We use the name as your display name and, if you agree on your first sign-in, copy the picture as your profile picture; you can change either at any time.
  • What you create: activities (names, descriptions, locations, prices, links, photos and your private labels), groups, plans on schedules and events.
  • Group activity: which groups you're in, invitations, who is going to which plan, and a history of changes (for example, who replaced an event with an activity).
  • Notifications about invitations and schedule clashes.
  • Server logs: like most online services, our hosting providers automatically keep standard logs of requests to the app (such as IP address, browser type and time) for a limited period, to keep the service secure and working.

We don't use advertising, sell data, or track you across other apps or websites.

Who can see it

  • Your username, display name, bio and profile picture are visible to other signed-in users of the app.
  • Activities are private until you share them into a group; then the group's members can see and edit them.
  • Plans, events and change history in a group are visible to that group's members.
  • Your email address isn't shown to others. People can find you by email only if they type it exactly.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with our hosting providers: Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage, in Singapore) and Netlify (the web app). Account emails, such as sign-up confirmations and password resets, are sent through Resend (in Japan), which receives your email address to deliver them. When you type a location (an activity's, or a place to measure from in Near…), or Fill from link suggests one, that text is sent to Google (Google Maps Platform) to suggest matching places; it isn't linked to your name or email, and we keep only the place you pick. To show how far activities are from a place (Near…), the places' IDs are sent to Google to find where they are; those coordinates are kept for at most 30 days. If you choose Use my location, your device's location is used on your device only and is never sent to us or stored. When you use Fill from link, the link, its public preview (title, caption, preview image), any screenshot or caption you add, and the names of your labels (so it can pick one) are sent through AIML API to Anthropic (Claude, an AI model) to suggest the activity's details; they aren't linked to your name or email, and only what you save is kept in EasyPlan. All of them process it only to run the service.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, pictures, labels and activities. Plans that used your activities remain for the other people in them as events that keep the activity's name as their title, groups you own pass to another member, and earlier changes in group history show as "a deleted user".

Children

EasyPlan isn't intended for children under 13.

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
